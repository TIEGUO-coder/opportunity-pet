# Creator Store Launch Kit Blueprint

This blueprint defines the product behavior before any MAH-specific formats are known. It is the business source of truth that a future MAH adapter must translate without changing the user promise.

## User input

The owner chooses one of two paths after the pet starts:

1. Try the included messy freelance-client sample.
2. Use a template, prompt pack, checklist, guide, workflow, or related digital asset that the owner may sell.

The route cannot begin without the source material, ownership confirmation, one target customer, one storefront, and a reviewable price range.

## Evidence gate

The pet should not present a generic AI idea as a qualified opportunity. A qualified Creator Store lead needs at least two recent public signals, source URLs, observation dates, and a reason the opportunity matches the owner's skills or assets.

## Fixed demonstration product

The only end-to-end demonstration product is the **Freelance Client Portal Kit** for freelancers and solo service businesses.

It contains:

- Client intake questionnaire
- Project progress template
- File-delivery checklist
- Reusable client email templates
- Quick-start guide PDF
- Storefront title and description
- Storefront FAQ
- Cover-image brief
- Downloadable ZIP package

## Business stages

1. Inspect the source asset.
2. Validate the opportunity.
3. Build the sellable package.
4. Review product quality.
5. Prepare the storefront listing.
6. Ask the owner to approve publishing.
7. Publish the approved listing and return a verifiable public URL.
8. Monitor product status, orders, refunds, and revenue.

## Recurring intents

- Opportunity scouting begins after a builder profile and scouting preferences exist.
- Storefront monitoring begins only after publishing returns a verifiable public product URL.

These are business intents, not guessed MAH commands. They will be mapped to the official MAH mechanisms after release.

## Definition of success

- Every promised deliverable exists in the final ZIP.
- The owner approved the product, price, listing, and public claims.
- Publishing returned a verifiable public product URL.
- The latest result identifies its source and check time.
- Illustrative revenue is never presented as verified revenue.
